If you’re testing this

  1. Start in the Card Generator. Leave the seed alone, set guests to 4, and hit generate.
  2. Print to PDF rather than paper — you just want to see that the cards look right.
  3. Open the DJ Console in a second tab and play a few songs. Snippets stream from Apple’s 30-second previews, so the first one may take a moment.
  4. After roughly a dozen songs someone will have a line. Use Verify winner with a card number to check it.
Music is never hosted here — snippets are Apple’s public 30‑second previews, or your own files, which stay on your machine.
