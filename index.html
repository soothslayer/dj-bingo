If you are testing this

  1. Start in Print the cards. Leave the seed alone, set guests to 4, and hit generate.
  2. Print to PDF rather than paper — you just want to see that the cards look right.
  3. Open the DJ console in a second tab and play a few songs. Snippets stream from Apple’s 30-second previews, so the first one may take a moment.
  4. Round 1 is one line, so somebody has it within about a dozen songs. Use Verify winner with a card number to check the claim.

The console and the big screen must both be open from this same address for the screen to follow the console automatically. Check the seed matches what is printed on the cards — ANNIVERSARY-50-6 unless you changed it. Music is never hosted here: snippets are Apple’s public 30‑second previews, or your own files, which stay on your machine. Full instructions.
